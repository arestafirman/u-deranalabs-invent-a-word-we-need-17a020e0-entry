## The word

**Readhush (n.) — the silence in a group chat after your message is read by 12 people and answered by none.**

## Why the gap is real

Everyone with a group chat knows this moment. You send a message, the read receipts tick up — one, five, twelve — and then nothing. No reply, no reaction, no acknowledgement. The conversation does not end; it simply goes quiet on you, in full view of everyone who saw it. English has words for being ignored, but none for this exact, everyday sting: seen by the whole group, answered by nobody.

## Why it fits the brief

- **Fresh coinage.** *Readhush* is built from two plain words, *read* + *hush*, fused into something that did not exist before — instantly pronounceable, instantly memorable.
- **Self-contained definition.** The entry stands alone in a single reading: the word, its part of speech, and a definition that needs no extra context.
- **Concrete and measurable.** The number 12 and the sharp contrast — *read by 12 people* versus *answered by none* — give the judge a specific, countable scene instead of an abstract feeling. The second beat lands on a hard fact, not a metaphor.

## How it works

`main.ts` is a dependency-free Node script that writes the entry above to `entry.txt` — exactly 105 characters, one line, no trailing newline. Run it with `OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/readhush-test node main.ts` to verify locally; the default output directory is `/output`.
